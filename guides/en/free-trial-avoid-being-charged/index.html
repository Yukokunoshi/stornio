Free-trial traps: how to not get charged

Most free trials turn into a paid subscription automatically on the last day. That is legal if you were told clearly. What costs money is forgetting the date or cancelling in the wrong place.

3 min read · Updated 2026-11-20

General information, not legal advice. National rules can differ and can change; check your country's official consumer information or a consumer advice body before you act.

Before you start the trial

  • Read what happens when the trial ends: the price, how often you pay and the first payment date.
  • Note the exact end date, not just "7 days".
  • Check how you can cancel: in the app, in your account, by e-mail or through the App Store or Google Play.
  • Watch for extra options you did not ask for.

Set a reminder before the last day

Set a reminder a few days before the trial ends, not on the day itself. Some services need time to process a cancellation, and Apple's terms say an auto-renewing subscription renews unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Cancel in the right place

A trial started through Apple or Google is cancelled in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it. A trial started on a company's website is cancelled in your account there or as its terms describe. Save the confirmation.

Extra payments need your express consent

Under the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU, Art. 22), a business needs your express consent for any payment beyond the main price. A pre-ticked box does not count. If you were charged for an add-on you never actively chose, you can ask for that money back.

How Stornio helps

Common questions

If I cancel a free trial early, do I lose the rest of the trial?

It depends on the service. Some let you use the trial until its end, others stop it immediately. The service's terms or the store's subscription page usually say which.

I forgot and was charged. Can I get the money back?

If you signed up online, check whether you are still within the 14-day withdrawal period of the contract, and ask the company. Many companies refund on request even when they do not have to.

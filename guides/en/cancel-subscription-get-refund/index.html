How to cancel a subscription and get a refund, step by step

Cancelling a subscription is usually simple once you know where you signed up and what the contract says. Getting money back is a separate question. Here is the order that saves the most time.

5 min read · Updated 2026-11-20

General information, not legal advice. National rules can differ and can change; check your country's official consumer information or a consumer advice body before you act.

1. Find out where you subscribed

Check how you pay. If the subscription is billed by Apple or Google, you cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, not in the app itself. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

If you pay the company directly, look for a cancellation option in your account, the cancellation address in the terms, or the customer service e-mail.

2. Check the terms

Before you cancel, find the following in the contract or the terms:

  • the next renewal or billing date
  • the notice period, if there is one
  • any minimum term
  • how the company accepts cancellations: online, by e-mail or by letter

3. Cancel in a way you can prove

Use the company's cancellation function if it has one, and save the confirmation. Otherwise send a short written cancellation by e-mail or letter with your name, customer number and the date. Ask for written confirmation of the cancellation and the end date.

In Germany, businesses that let you take out a subscription online must also offer a cancellation button on their website (§ 312k BGB).

4. When you can ask for money back

If the subscription is new and you signed up online or by phone, you can usually withdraw within 14 days of the contract without a reason. If you asked for the service to start during that time, you may have to pay for the part you used.

After the 14 days, cancelling normally stops future payments but does not give back what you already paid. Whether you get a partial refund depends on the contract. You can always ask, especially if you were charged after the end date or for something you did not agree to.

5. Check the next billing date

After the end date, check your bank or card statement. If you were charged again, write to the company with your proof of cancellation and ask for the money back. If you pay by direct debit, your bank can also help.

Common questions

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. A subscription billed by Apple or Google stays active until you cancel it in the store's subscription settings.

Can I get a refund for the rest of the month?

It depends on the contract. EU law does not give a general right to a pro-rata refund after the 14-day withdrawal period, but some companies offer one, so ask.

Is an e-mail enough to cancel?

Often yes, if the terms allow cancellation by e-mail. Check the terms, and keep the sent e-mail and any reply.

Template: letter to cancel a subscription

A cancellation letter only needs a few things: who you are, which contract, that you cancel it, from when, and a request for confirmation. Copy the template below and fill in the parts in brackets.

3 min read · Updated 2026-11-20

General information, not legal advice. National rules can differ and can change; check your country's official consumer information or a consumer advice body before you act.

Cancellation letter

Subject: Cancellation of my subscription – [customer number]

Dear Sir or Madam,

I hereby cancel my subscription to [service name], customer or contract number [number], at the earliest possible date [or: on [date]].

Please confirm the cancellation and the date the contract ends in writing. Please do not charge me after that date. If I pay by direct debit, my authorisation to debit my account ends on the same date.

Name: [your name]

Address: [your address]

E-mail used for the account: [e-mail]

Date: [date]

Withdrawal within 14 days (EU model wording)

If the contract is new and you concluded it online, by phone or away from the business's premises, you can withdraw within 14 days. The EU model withdrawal form reads:

I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract of sale of the following goods / for the provision of the following service: [description]

Ordered on [date] / received on [date]

Name of consumer: [your name]

Address of consumer: [your address]

Signature of consumer (only if this form is sent on paper)

Date: [date]

How to send it

  • Use the method the terms name: e-mail, an online form or post.
  • For an important cancellation by post, use a method that gives you proof of sending.
  • Keep a copy of the letter and the proof of sending.
  • Note the date by which you expect a reply, and check your statement after the end date.

Common questions

Do I have to give a reason for cancelling?

Not for a normal cancellation under the contract or for a withdrawal within 14 days.

Does the letter need a signature?

An e-mail usually does not. For a letter on paper, sign it.

Subscription tracker and cancellation letter maker. No account, no bank connection, all data stays on your phone.

Support

Questions or found a bug? Write to skayiskir@hotmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Your data is on your device, so we cannot see it – please do not send invoices or personal data when reporting a problem.

FAQ

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server storing it. Under Settings › Data and backup you can create an encrypted backup or delete everything.

Does the app give legal advice?

No. Stornio builds letters from ready-made templates; you decide whether to send them. We do not act on your behalf.

How does the AI work?

It is off by default. It only works after separate consent, and before every request we show you the redacted text, which you can edit.
